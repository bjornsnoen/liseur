Translate selected passages or a whole book page by page. Page translations are saved on your device and return when you reopen a book without another request. The reader follows your place and restores the original text when you stop. Read-aloud resumes near where you paused. Dictionary lookups can show base-word meanings and Wiktionary translations in another language. On wide screens, Settings can stay beside its pages. The footer is locked by default; hold it to change what it shows.
