This release adds an on-device Codex for the book you’re reading and a spoiler-free Reading companion for profiles and chapter recaps. You can also translate and listen to pages using local servers, and the bookshelf widget is back.

### Reading

- Introducting Codex, a better way to to find people, places and groups, read their passages, and see who appears together in  a book. It only shows chapters before your place, works offline, and its map supports TalkBack and keyboard navigation. ([#347](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/347))

- Select a name to see a spoiler-free profile with its source passages. The Reading companion can also recap chapters to help with later lookups. It uses the passages before your place and sends them to the service and model you choose. ([#332](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/332), [#339](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/339))

- Translate pages with earlier text from the same chapter for context, look up translated words, and read translated pages aloud with the page and speech kept in sync. Translation can use Ollama, LM Studio or llama.cpp; read aloud can use Kokoro-FastAPI. ([#323](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/323), [#324](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/324), [#328](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/328), [#339](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/339))

- See input and output prices when choosing translation models from DeepInfra or Together AI. ([#330](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/330))
- Clear saved translations on your device or, with a compatible liseur-sync server, from your account. Translations can also be reused on another device through liseur-sync. ([#326](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/326), [#329](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/329))
- Choose On, Off or Reversed for volume-key paging. In Reversed mode, volume up turns pages forward and volume down goes back. ([#335](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/335), [#336](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/336))

### Library

- Bring the bookshelf widget back to your home screen. It puts your current book first, followed by recently opened and unread books. ([#333](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/333))

  <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e873653a-9063-4412-9af1-35219850ab57" alt="Bookshelf widget in light mode" width="320">
  <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/55e0208d-ae83-408b-8bf9-6b1a82df2f36" alt="Bookshelf widget in dark mode" width="320">

- Include completed companion recaps in ZIP backups, and optionally include account details and passwords protected by a backup password. Other backup contents, including settings and service addresses, remain unencrypted. ([#338](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/338))
- Reading sessions shorter than two minutes no longer appear as separate entries in statistics; their time still counts toward totals and charts. ([#325](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/325))

### Fixes

- Narrow margins now use more of the available width on tablets. ([#327](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/327))
